Fix merged (v0.98.43 unit guard, v0.98.45 the `regression.sh` sibling) and is
correct. **What is unresolved is whether the TEST guards the right thing.**

`HS-27` pins the **capacity** path (200 KB fillers, threshold at the 64 KB pipe
buffer). The mechanism that can bite a real ~1-2 KB manifest is **scheduling**,
measured at **13/14500 (~0.09%)** under load vs 0/5000 idle. Different paths.

**The ~4% figure in merge commit `79cca763` is UNSUPPORTED** — 40-100x too
high, unreproducible even under a real parallel `make` (0/3000). Do not cite it.

**Right answer is the redesign**: membership checks that never shell out make
both paths structurally impossible, instead of racing to test two failure modes
of an idiom that should not be there. Full write-up in the Task 88 section of
`.prompts/2026-07-18.md`.

### Task 90: extended-keys NR 0xB0 / 0xB1 / NR 0x68 bit 4 — wired at NEITHER end

Github issue **#33** (milestone v1.0, bug).
